Neurosciences Ph.D. Interview Week

We are excited to welcome prospective students for Ph.D. Interview Week, which will take place February 17 - 21, 2025. This is a crucial step in the admissions process, where selected applicants will have the opportunity to meet our faculty, explore our campus, and attend the Neuroscience symposium at the Salk Institute for Biological Studies.

Key Information:

  • Dates: February 17 - 21, 2025
  • Format: In-Person Interviews and Events
  • Travel Arrangements: The graduate program will assist in Travel and Accommodations. The program will cover expenses at a fixed amount
  • Interview Invitations: Invitations to interview will be sent out in early January 2025.

During the interview week, you will engage in one-on-one and group sessions with faculty, learn about ongoing research, and meet with current Ph.D. students. This is a unique opportunity to see if our program aligns with your academic and professional goals.

We look forward to meeting you and sharing more about what makes our program a leading choice for neuroscience Ph.D. training.
